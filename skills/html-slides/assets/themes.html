Themes

Tell the assistant the name of the theme you want (for example "use warm"). In a deck source it is set with theme: name. The slides shown are the Japanese sample deck.

lightLight: blue on white. The safe choice.

darkDark: navy background. Screen sharing, technical topics.

corporateCorporate: navy and gold, square corners. Formal business documents.

warmWarm: cream background, serif headings. Stories and calm reading.

forestForest: soft green. Environment, health, education.

sakuraSakura: pale pink, round corners. Gentle announcements.

monoMono: black and white with a yellow highlighter. Bold and minimal.

popPop: pink and cyan on cream, rounded type. Friendly introductions.

neonNeon: cyan and pink on black. Tech and games.

chalkChalk: blackboard green, textbook typeface. Lessons and study groups.
